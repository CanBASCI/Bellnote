# Bellnote Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026.

Bellnote is made by the developer at [github.com/CanBASCI](https://github.com/CanBASCI). This policy says what Bellnote does with your information. The short version: **Bellnote does not collect your information. It stays on your iPhone.**

## The short version

- No account, no sign-in, no sign-up.
- Your audio, transcripts, notes, files, timetable, scores and quiz answers are stored only on your iPhone.
- Nothing is uploaded to the developer or to any server of ours. We do not have a server.
- No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDK that collects data.
- The AI runs on your iPhone. Your recordings and notes are never sent to an AI service.

## What is stored on your iPhone

Bellnote keeps these in its own storage on your iPhone, so the app can work:

- Recordings you make, or audio files you choose to load.
- The transcript, summary, notes and homework made from a recording.
- Photos you take for a board or a schedule, and files you add to a course (PDF, images, slides).
- Your courses, timetable, exam dates, scores, assignments, quiz questions and answers.
- Lecturer details you type (name, phone, email). Bellnote does not look anyone up.
- Your settings, such as language, appearance and font.
- A record that you confirmed you may record a class: the two statements, the time, the app version, and whether Face ID succeeded. It stays with that recording and is deleted with it.

This information never leaves your iPhone through Bellnote. Like other apps, the app's storage can be part of a device backup if you have turned backups on in iOS. That is your choice in iOS settings, not something Bellnote does.

## The one network use: downloading models

Bellnote does not include its AI models, so the app stays small. When **you tap Download** on a model, Bellnote downloads that model's files from public repositories on [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co). Nothing downloads without your tap.

- The request to download is an ordinary web request. Hugging Face can see your IP address and the standard information any download carries. Its handling of that is described in the [Hugging Face privacy policy](https://huggingface.co/privacy).
- Bellnote sends no information about you, your recordings or your notes in these requests.
- The downloaded files are model weights, which are data. They are not code.
- After the models are installed, Bellnote works without a network.

Apple's on-device speech assets for dictation come from the system, not from Hugging Face.

## Permissions

Bellnote asks for each permission when you first use the feature. If you say no, only that feature is off.

| Permission | Why | Where the data goes |
|---|---|---|
| Microphone | Record a class | Saved on your iPhone |
| Camera | A board photo during a recording, or reading a schedule you frame | Saved on your iPhone |
| Face ID or passcode | Optional lock for your notes. It is on by default and can be turned off in Settings | Handled by iOS. Bellnote never sees your face or passcode |
| Speech recognition | Dictating a question to the assistant, on the device | Processed on your iPhone |
| Notifications | Optional reminders before class, and a note that a recording is ready | Local notifications. Nothing is sent |

Bellnote can keep recording when the screen is locked, using background audio, so a class is not lost. While it records, a recording indicator and a Live Activity are shown. Recording never starts without your tap.

## Recording other people

You decide when to record. Before the first recording of a course, Bellnote asks you to confirm that you are allowed to record the class and that the lecturer or school allows it. The laws and rules about recording differ by country and school. **You are responsible for recording only where it is allowed.** Bellnote is for personal study notes, not for sharing recordings.

## Sharing, selling and tracking

Bellnote does not sell, share or rent your information. It does not track you across apps or websites and does not use the advertising identifier. You can share a course file yourself with the iOS share sheet. That is your action, and what happens next is up to the app you share to.

## Children

Bellnote does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is yours and on your iPhone. You can delete a recording, a lecture, a course, a file or a model inside the app. Deleting a model does not delete your notes. Deleting Bellnote from your iPhone removes its data. Because the developer holds none of it, there is nothing to request from us.
