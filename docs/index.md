# Bellnote

Bellnote is a study app for iPhone. You record a class you are allowed to record, and Bellnote turns it into a transcript, a summary, notes and homework. Every point links to the minute it was said, so you can hear it again.

Everything happens on your iPhone. There is no account, no upload and no analytics.

## What it does

- **Record a class.** Pause, resume and stop, even from the Lock Screen. Recording only starts after you confirm that you are allowed to record.
- **Notes you can trace.** Each recording becomes a transcript, a summary, notes and homework. Tap a point to hear its minute.
- **Timetable and exams.** Weekly slots, exam dates, scores and reminders before class.
- **Files and quizzes.** Keep slides and PDFs with each course, then study with questions made from them.
- **Ask and listen.** Ask about a course by voice or text, or have a lecture read back to you.
- **Private.** Audio, notes and the AI stay on the device, behind Face ID if you like.

Bellnote is available in English and Turkish.
